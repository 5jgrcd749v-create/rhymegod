Rhyme God — privacy policy

Rhyme God collects no data.

Everything runs on your device. There are no accounts, analytics, ads or trackers, and the app never connects to the internet. On the Mac it isn't allowed to: the app is sandboxed with outgoing connections switched off. On Android it asks for no permissions at all, including internet access.

What you type

Search terms are never saved or sent anywhere. Words you set aside while writing are held in memory only and are gone when the app closes. A word reaches your clipboard only when you copy it.

Settings

Your theme and display preferences are stored on your device. They are never sent to us — there is nowhere for them to be sent to. If you back your device up to iCloud or to a computer, they are included in that backup, the same as any other app's settings. On Android they are excluded from system backups entirely.

Children

The app collects nothing from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted here and in the app under the cog, in "about".
