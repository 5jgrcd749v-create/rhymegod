# Rhyme God's word list

`lexicon.tsv` is the pronunciation list that Rhyme God searches, as shipped in Rhyme God 1.2.1
(build 6) for iPhone, iPad and Mac. It is published here because it is an adaptation of works
licensed CC BY-SA 4.0, which asks that adaptations be shared under the same terms. `glossary.tsv`,
the dictionary and thesaurus behind the app's meaning card, is published beside it. Keep this
notice with the files.

## What `lexicon.tsv` is made from

1. **The CMU Pronouncing Dictionary** — Copyright (C) 1993-2015 Carnegie Mellon University.
   https://github.com/cmusphinx/cmudict. Most of the pronunciations. BSD-style licence, reproduced
   in full below.
2. **FrequencyWords** by Hermit Dave — https://github.com/hermitdave/FrequencyWords — lists
   licensed CC BY-SA 4.0. How common each word is, which decides whether a word is kept and the rank
   it carries. The counts come from the OpenSubtitles corpus (https://www.opensubtitles.org/) as
   distributed by OPUS (https://opus.nlpl.eu/).
3. **English Wiktionary** — https://en.wiktionary.org/ — written by its contributors and licensed
   CC BY-SA 4.0 (also available under the GNU Free Documentation License), gathered with WikiPron
   (https://github.com/CUNY-CL/wikipron; Lee et al., "Massively multilingual pronunciation mining
   with WikiPron", LREC 2020). Pronunciations for words the CMU dictionary never had: emoji, ramen,
   rizz, cosplay.
4. **Rhyme God's own additions** — © 2026 Selfish Pursuits, licensed CC BY-SA 4.0 like the rest:
   a short hand-written list of words newer than both dictionaries (vape, tiktok, gaslighting) and
   of sung stresses added beside a word's spoken one (HEL-lo beside hel-LO).

## What was changed

Words were ranked by frequency and the rare ones dropped; names and modern coinages were tagged;
slurs were removed. Wiktionary's pronunciations were converted from IPA to the CMU dictionary's
ARPAbet notation, and their stress was worked out from the vowels, since Wiktionary's published
lists carry no stress marks. The hand-written words and sung stresses were added. WordNet was cut
to the words in this list, with at most four meanings a word and only synonyms that are themselves
in the list.

`Scripts/build_lexicon.py` and `Scripts/build_glossary.py` in the Rhyme God repository build the
two files and document each step.

## Format

`lexicon.tsv`: one pronunciation per line, tab separated:

```
word <TAB> rank <TAB> flags <TAB> ARPAbet phones
```

`rank` is the frequency rank, lower being more common. `flags` is `n` for a name, `m` for a modern
or slang word, empty otherwise.

`glossary.tsv`: one sense per line, in WordNet's order (most common first), tab separated:

```
word <TAB> part of speech (n, v, adj, adv) <TAB> definition <TAB> synonyms, comma-separated <TAB> antonyms
```
